The Challenge

Business Problem

The Core Question

Amazon generates $9.25M in global e-commerce revenue, yet hidden inefficiencies in logistics, pricing, payment processing, and inventory allocation quietly erode profitability. Where exactly is the business leaking value—and how much is recoverable?

Supply Chain Bottlenecks

Expedited shipping costs more but delivers late. Which couriers, routes, and fulfillment methods are failing customers?

Hidden Cost Erosion

Shipping fees, payment processing costs, and refunds consume $978K. Which categories and regions are unprofitable?

Discount Inefficiency

Are discount strategies actually driving volume, or just giving away margin on products that would sell at full price?

Inventory Misallocation

74.7% of products are "Bleeders" — selling volume at thin margins. Which SKUs deserve more capital investment?

Deep Dive Analysis

Key Dashboards & Insights

Each dashboard targets a critical business domain, surfacing actionable findings from the data.

Dashboard 4

Logistics & Supply Chain

Identifying bottlenecks in the delivery network that drive late deliveries, refunds, and poor customer experience across 14 markets.

Late Rate: 12–19% Avg Delivery: 3.1 days $398K Refund Exposure
Critical Finding

Expedited shipping is the most expensive AND the least reliable option — with 12–19% late delivery rates across markets. FBM Expedited in the US fails on nearly 1 in 5 orders. UPS (8.75% late) and USPS (7.73%) are the worst performers.

Dashboard 5

Profitability & Margin Erosion

Tracing the journey from $9.25M gross revenue to true net profit, exposing three hidden cost layers that erode margins.

$978K Hidden Costs Books: -231.8% Margin UPI: 203.7% Fee Rate
Critical Finding

$978K in hidden costs (shipping $346K, payment fees $233K, refunds $399K) silently erode revenue. UPI and NetBanking payment methods cost more than they generate — losing money on every single transaction. South America and Asia Books operate at catastrophic negative margins.

Dashboard 6

Sales & Revenue Optimization

Evaluating which markets, discount strategies, and time periods drive the most profitable growth to focus marketing & sales efforts.

US Margin: Only 9.1% EU Margin: 21–24% 49% Sep Volume Drop
Critical Finding

The US generates 29.6% of revenue but at only 9.1% margin — less than half of any European market. The 5–10% discount tier is completely unnecessary: it delivers identical margins to full price with zero incremental volume, wasting an estimated $100K–150K annually.

Dashboard 7

Customer Behavior & Loyalty

Quantifying the Prime vs. Non-Prime value gap and mapping optimal payment method infrastructure across 14 countries.

Prime AOV: +18.6% Refund: 3.01% vs 2.99% 3 Checkout Profiles
Critical Finding

Prime members spend 18.6% more per order ($261 vs. $220) by choosing higher-value products — with virtually identical return rates (3.01% vs. 2.99%). Every Prime conversion is pure upside. India and Brazil depend on local payment methods (UPI, Pix) for 60%+ of revenue.

Dashboard 8

Product & Inventory Strategy

Classifying 198 SKUs into Hero/Average/Bleeder quadrants and analyzing variant-level performance to optimize inventory purchasing.

74.7% Bleeders 35 Hero SKUs Defect Rate: 3.0%
Critical Finding

74.7% of all products are Bleeders — selling volume at below-average profitability. Only 35 products are "Heroes," and 22 of those are Gaming. Five entire categories have zero Hero products. Pink is the #1 selling variant across 6 of 10 brands analyzed.

Technical Approach

Methodology

Phase 1: Data Cleaning

Microsoft Excel

  • Handled null values in FX rates, phone numbers, and refund amounts
  • Standardized date formats across 14 country locales (ISO 8601)
  • Created calculated columns: Order Tier, Delivery Performance, Late Flag
  • Engineered date hierarchies: Year, Month, Quarter, Day of Week

Phase 2: SQL Analysis

PostgreSQL / SQL Server

  • CTEs for Month-over-Month and Year-over-Year growth calculations
  • Window functions (RANK, LAG, running totals) for trend analysis
  • CASE-based segmentation for discount tiers, order tiers, and delivery performance
  • Complex aggregations: margin erosion waterfalls, category × region matrices

Phase 3: Visualization

Tableau

  • 5 interactive dashboards with KPI cards and drill-down filters
  • Advanced chart types: Pareto, waterfall, dumbbell, scatter quadrants
  • Heatmaps for country × fulfillment × shipping method analysis
  • Geographic treemaps with margin-colored encoding over revenue sizing
